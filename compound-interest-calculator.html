FAQ

What is the rule of 72?

Divide 72 by your annual return to approximate how many years your money doubles. At 8%, that is about 9 years.

Does monthly compounding beat quarterly?

Yes, at the same nominal rate — more frequent compounding means a higher effective yield (8% compounded monthly yields 8.30% effectively).

What is effective annual rate?

The real yearly growth after compounding. It is always equal to or above the quoted nominal rate whenever compounding happens more than once a year.
